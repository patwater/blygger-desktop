section: Added

- **Responses: read a response beside the list.** ⏎ on a response (or a
  click on who responded) opens its post in a reading pane beside the
  Responses list: the Reader's own pane, with its text, version pill and
  action row, so a response can be read, quoted or replied to without
  leaving the screen. A post your blyg doesn't hold is fetched from its
  author's public item document, as a quote's original is. While the pane
  is open, j/k carry it to the next response; esc closes it, and esc again
  leaves. A response from a web page that isn't a blyg post opens in the
  browser, with a toast saying so. **o** still opens any response in the
  browser (↗). `crates/blyg-app/src/reading/mentions.rs`.
